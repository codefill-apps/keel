Apps

Every app, ranked. Every opening, counted.

See where the time went, today, over 7 days or over 30. Each app shows its real icon, the kind of time it belongs to, and how many times you opened it.

  • Kind of time. If Keel guesses an app’s kind wrong, change it.
  • Daily limit. A gentle reminder when you pass the time you set. It never blocks the app.
  • Opened N times. Often the number that surprises people more than the minutes.

Your run

Days in a row. Time given back.

Keel keeps a run of days within your budget, with room for the odd off day. Under it, “Time back” adds up how much less you spent on your phone than a usual day would have taken.

  • Rest days. One missed day a week doesn’t count, and doesn’t break the run.
  • Time back. Measured against your own first week of history, not someone else’s average.
